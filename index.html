An orange cat wakes in a sun-soaked feline heaven and sails between tropical islands, easing one small regret after another — searching for the way home. Here, even goodbye can be gentle.

關於我們About us

我們相信，告別也能是一段溫柔、幽默、帶著療癒感的旅程。

We believe even a farewell can be tender, funny, and quietly healing.

橡實洞工作室是一間紮根台灣的療癒系內容工作室。我們橫跨 XR 沉浸式體驗、行動遊戲與多媒體互動，把在地的情感紋理，化為一個個可以鑽進去歇息的小宇宙。小小的、慢慢的、可愛的，但對待每一份情感都很認真。

Acorn Den Studio is a healing-focused content studio rooted in Taiwan, working across XR experiences, mobile games, and multimedia. We turn local emotional texture into little worlds you can burrow into and rest — small, slow, adorable, yet serious about every feeling we hold.
