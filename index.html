My Cat Became a Sea Travel Guide… After Death!?

死後來到貓咪天國的橘貓，駕著小船穿梭熱帶群島，抓魚！吃魚！拉拉其他貓咪的尾巴！在化解一個個小小遺憾的過程中，努力揚帆尋找回到主人身邊的方法——在貓咪的世界裡，有沒有可能，我們不用說再見？

An orange cat arrives in feline heaven and sets sail among tropical islands. Catch fish! Eat fish! Tug other cats' tails! Easing one small regret after another, he sails on in search of a way back to his owner — in the world of cats, is it possible that we never have to say goodbye?

When the Flowers Bloom

末日之後，人類遷居地底，並陷入沉睡。植物研究員芽帶著能淨化空氣的植物，踏上重返地表的旅程。一路上，她追逐摯友的身影，重新拾起共同許下的約定，邁開腳步，讓花重新盛開在荒原之中，在陽光下野餐。

After the end of the world, humanity retreats underground and falls into a long sleep. Ya, a botanist carrying air-purifying plants, journeys back to the surface — chasing the shadow of her dearest friend, picking up a promise they once made, and letting flowers bloom across the wasteland again, until they can picnic in the sun.

Tales of the Evening Primrose

1930 年代的港都咖啡館裡，女給夕顏與來客宮野先生悄悄相戀。玩家將與朋友們一同化身女給參與這段短暫而珍貴的相遇，在曖昧、告別與未竟心願之間，在大船離港之前，感受一期一會的夏日戀情。

In a 1930s harbor-town café, the waitress Yugao and a visiting guest, Mr. Miyano, quietly fall in love. You and your friends step into the roles of café girls of that era, sharing this brief, precious encounter — drifting between flirtation, farewell, and unspoken wishes, savoring a once-in-a-lifetime summer romance before the great ship leaves port.

The Island of Shells貝殼島

The Shell of Island

改編自白色恐怖政治受難者陳欽生的真實經歷，以皮影戲與互動 VR 呈現小男孩追尋貝殼島的童話故事。與母親告別，跟隨藍鳥走進森林、收集發光羽毛，找到森林盡頭的真相。在希望與謊言交錯之間，重新找到家的航向。

Adapted from the true story of Chen Chin-sheng, a survivor of Taiwan's White Terror, this shadow-puppet interactive VR work tells the fairy tale of a boy searching for the Island of Shells. Bidding his mother farewell, he follows a blue bird into the forest, gathering glowing feathers to reach the truth at its end — and, where hope and lies intertwine, finds a new heading for home.

橡實洞工作室是一間紮根於南臺灣的 XR 敘事工作室。我們創作 XR 沉浸式體驗、行動遊戲與多媒體互動，將在地文化與細膩情感，化為一段段值得駐足的旅程。

讓每一次相遇、每一份友誼、每一個選擇，都能在人心裡留下微小卻長久的力量。

Acorn Den Studio is an XR storytelling studio rooted in southern Taiwan.

We craft immersive XR experiences, mobile games, and interactive media, weaving local culture and delicate emotion into journeys worth lingering in — so that every encounter, every friendship, and every choice leaves a small but lasting strength in the heart.
