隱私權政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 21 日

橡實洞遊戲有限公司（下稱我們，或橡實洞）重視使用者（下稱您）的隱私權。本隱私權政策（下稱本政策）說明您使用 iPhone 遊戲《口袋柴犬》（下稱本遊戲）時，我們蒐集哪些資料、如何利用與保護這些資料，以及您所享有的權利。本政策依中華民國《個人資料保護法》（下稱個資法）及相關法規訂定。當您下載或使用本遊戲，即表示您已閱讀、瞭解並同意本政策之內容。

本遊戲不需要註冊帳號或登入，不含廣告，不蒐集廣告識別碼（IDFA），亦不會跨應用程式追蹤。我們並未架設遊戲伺服器，遊戲進度儲存於您的手機及您本人的 iCloud。

蒐集資料的種類及利用方式

計步資料：經您同意後，本遊戲會透過 iPhone 的「動作與健身」功能（CoreMotion）讀取每日步數，用以換算為遊戲獎勵。步數僅於您的手機上處理，不會傳送給我們或任何第三方。

遊戲進度：遊戲進度（包括您於紀錄頁選填、用於估算卡路里的體重）均儲存於您的手機。若您於設定頁開啟「iCloud 同步」，遊戲進度將透過 Apple 的 iCloud（Key-Value 儲存）同步至您本人的 iCloud，以便於更換手機或重新安裝時找回進度。我們無法讀取您 iCloud 中的資料。

購買資訊：App 內購買由 Apple 的 App 內購買服務（StoreKit）處理，付款資訊由 Apple 處理，我們無法取得。本遊戲會於手機上記錄交易編號，以避免同一筆購買重複發放。

當機與錯誤資料：本遊戲使用 Google 的 Firebase Crashlytics 蒐集當機報告、錯誤紀錄、裝置型號、iOS 版本、App 版本、當機時的裝置狀態（例如可用記憶體與儲存空間）、當機前的遊戲操作紀錄（例如完成新手教學的步驟），以及 Crashlytics 安裝識別碼（用於計算受影響的使用者人數），用以找出並修正錯誤。

遊戲使用情形：本遊戲使用 Unity Gaming Services 的 Unity Analytics 蒐集遊戲內的行為事件（例如完成教學的步驟、散步分鐘數、開啟商店、購買結果、兌換碼的兌換結果，不包含兌換碼本身）、購買交易紀錄、開啟與關閉遊戲的時間、裝置資訊（例如機型、作業系統版本）、由 IP 位址推估的國家或地區，以及 Unity 產生的安裝識別碼（並非廣告識別碼），用以瞭解玩家在遊戲中遇到的困難，並據以改善遊戲。

遠端設定：本遊戲會自我們的網站（架設於 GitHub Pages）讀取遊戲公告、更新提示與兌換碼資訊。此過程與一般瀏覽網頁相同，網站主機 GitHub 可能記錄您的 IP 位址等連線資訊。

聯絡我們：當您於設定頁點選「聯絡我們」寄信時，我們將收到您的電子郵件地址、信件內容，以及信件中自動附上的診斷資訊，包括時間、App 版本、iOS 版本、機型、隨機產生的裝置 id、iCloud 同步狀態、存檔狀態、狗狗幣數量、遊玩天數及內購交易筆數。診斷資訊不包含姓名、狗狗名稱、Apple 帳號或位置，寄出前您可自行檢視並修改。前述「隨機產生的裝置 id」係由本遊戲於手機上產生，並非 Apple 的裝置識別碼，無法用以識別您本人。上述資料僅用於回覆您的問題及排查錯誤。

通知：經您同意後，本遊戲會於手機上排定運動提醒通知。通知由手機本身發送，不經過任何伺服器。

利用之期間、地區、對象及方式

期間：依本政策「資料保存」所列之期限。

地區：中華民國，以及本政策「第三方服務」所列服務供應商之伺服器所在地，可能包括美國等境外地區。

對象：我們，以及受我們委託處理資料之第三方服務供應商。

方式：以自動化方式進行蒐集、傳輸、儲存及統計分析；客服信件由專人閱讀與回覆。

第三方服務

本遊戲使用下列第三方服務，各服務依其隱私權政策處理資料：

  • Apple iCloud 與 App 內購買：同步遊戲進度、處理付款。請參閱 Apple 隱私權政策；
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics：當機與錯誤回報。請參閱 Firebase 隱私權與安全性說明；
  • Unity Analytics（Unity Gaming Services）：遊戲使用情形分析。請參閱 Unity 隱私權政策；
  • GitHub Pages：存放我們的網站與遊戲公告設定檔。請參閱 GitHub 隱私權聲明。

個人資料的揭露

我們不會出售您的資料，亦不會將資料提供予廣告商或資料商。但依法令規定或主管機關要求提供者，不在此限。

安全性

本遊戲與 Firebase、Unity 及我們網站之間的連線，均使用 HTTPS 加密傳輸，我們亦僅於本政策所列之目的範圍內利用資料。若發生您的個人資料遭竊取、洩漏、竄改或其他侵害之情事，我們將依個資法第 12 條之規定，於查明後以適當方式通知您。

資料保存

手機上的遊戲進度：保存至您刪除本遊戲，或於設定頁使用「刪除紀錄」重新開始為止。

iCloud 上的遊戲進度：儲存於您本人的 iCloud。於設定頁使用「刪除紀錄」時將一併清除，亦可於 iPhone 的 iCloud 設定中自行管理。

當機與錯誤資料：由 Firebase Crashlytics 保存 90 天後開始刪除。

遊戲使用情形：Unity Analytics 的原始事件資料保存 13 個月後自動刪除；由前述資料統計而成、無法識別個人之數據（例如每日玩家人數），可能保存較長期間。

客服信件：保存至您的問題處理完畢；您亦得隨時要求我們刪除。

您所享有的權利

依個資法第 3 條之規定，您得向我們行使下列權利：

  • 查詢或請求閱覽；
  • 請求製給複製本；
  • 請求補充或更正；
  • 請求停止蒐集、處理或利用；
  • 請求刪除。

如需行使上述權利，請來信 support@acornden.com。依個資法第 13 條之規定，我們將於 15 日內處理查詢、閱覽及製給複製本之請求，並於 30 日內處理補充、更正、停止蒐集處理利用及刪除之請求；必要時得延長一次，延長期間分別不逾 15 日及 30 日，並將以書面告知您延長之原因。

由於本遊戲不設帳號，Firebase 與 Unity 中的資料係以隨機產生的安裝識別碼記錄，並未與您的姓名或電子郵件地址連結，因此我們通常無法辨識哪些資料屬於您。這些資料將於本政策「資料保存」所列期限屆滿後自動刪除。如您仍希望提出請求，我們將回覆可協助的範圍與方式。

此外，您亦可隨時自行採取下列措施：於 iPhone 的「設定」→「隱私權與安全性」→「動作與健身」關閉計步權限，或於「設定」→「通知」關閉通知；於紀錄頁清除已填寫的體重；於本遊戲設定頁關閉 iCloud 同步，或使用「刪除紀錄」清除遊戲進度；或刪除本遊戲，本遊戲即不再蒐集任何資料。

不提供資料之影響

您可以拒絕本遊戲讀取步數或發送通知，仍可正常遊玩，惟將無法以步數換取遊戲獎勵，亦不會收到運動提醒。體重為選填資料，未填寫時，卡路里將以預設值估算。若未開啟 iCloud 同步，刪除本遊戲或更換手機後，將無法找回遊戲進度。

未成年人之保護

本遊戲不會要求任何使用者提供姓名、電子郵件地址等可識別身分之資料，本政策「蒐集資料的種類及利用方式」所列資料之處理方式，適用於所有年齡的使用者。未成年人進行 App 內購買前，應先取得家長（或監護人）之同意；家長可使用 Apple 的「購買前詢問」與「螢幕使用時間」功能管理購買。若您是家長，並發現孩子曾透過「聯絡我們」向我們提供個人資料，請來信通知，我們將予以刪除。

本政策之變更

我們可能因法令或服務內容之變更而修訂本政策，修訂後將公告於本頁並更新上方日期。如有重大變更（例如開始蒐集新的資料類別），我們亦將透過遊戲內公告通知您。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Acorn Den Studio (橡實洞遊戲有限公司, hereinafter “we” or “Acorn Den”) respects the privacy of its users (hereinafter “you”). This Privacy Policy (hereinafter “this Policy”) explains what data we collect when you use the iPhone game Pocket Shiba (hereinafter “the Game”), how we use and protect that data, and the rights you have. This Policy is based on the Personal Data Protection Act of the Republic of China (Taiwan) (hereinafter “PDPA”) and related regulations. By downloading or using the Game, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Policy. If there is any discrepancy between the English and Chinese versions, the Chinese version shall prevail.

The Game does not require an account or sign-in, contains no ads, does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not track you across apps. We do not operate game servers; your progress is stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

Information We Collect and How We Use It

Step count: With your permission, the Game reads your daily step count through iPhone’s Motion & Fitness feature (CoreMotion) to convert it into in-game rewards. Step data is processed only on your iPhone and is never sent to us or any third party.

Game progress: Your game progress, including any body weight you choose to enter on the Records page for calorie estimates, is stored on your iPhone. If you turn on “iCloud Sync” in Settings, your progress is synced to your own iCloud through Apple’s iCloud key-value storage, so that you can recover it when you change phones or reinstall the Game. We cannot read the data in your iCloud.

Purchase information: In-app purchases are processed by Apple’s In-App Purchase service (StoreKit). Your payment information is handled by Apple and is never available to us. The Game records transaction IDs on your iPhone to prevent the same purchase from being granted twice.

Crash and error data: The Game uses Google’s Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports, error logs, device model, iOS version, app version, device state at the time of a crash (such as available memory and storage), a log of in-game actions before a crash (such as tutorial steps completed), and a Crashlytics installation identifier (used to count the number of affected users), in order to find and fix bugs.

Gameplay usage: The Game uses Unity Analytics, part of Unity Gaming Services, to collect in-game events (such as tutorial steps completed, walk length in minutes, opening the store, purchase results, and redeem code results, not including the code itself), purchase transaction records, the times the Game is opened and closed, device information (such as model and OS version), country or region estimated from your IP address, and an installation identifier generated by Unity (not the advertising identifier), in order to understand where players run into difficulties and improve the Game.

Remote configuration: The Game downloads game announcements, update notices, and redeem code information from our website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with visiting any website, the host, GitHub, may log connection information such as your IP address.

Contacting us: When you email us through “Contact Us” in Settings, we receive your email address, your message, and the diagnostic information automatically attached to it, including the time, app version, iOS version, device model, a randomly generated device ID, iCloud sync status, save file status, Doggy Coin balance, number of days played, and number of in-app purchase transactions. The diagnostic information does not include your name, your dog’s name, your Apple Account, or your location, and you may review and edit it before sending. The “randomly generated device ID” is created by the Game on your iPhone; it is not an Apple device identifier and cannot be used to identify you personally. This information is used only to respond to you and troubleshoot problems.

Notifications: With your permission, the Game schedules exercise reminder notifications on your iPhone. Notifications are delivered by your iPhone itself and do not go through any server.

Period, Region, Recipients, and Method of Use

Period: As set out in “Data Retention” below.

Region: The Republic of China (Taiwan) and the locations of the servers of the service providers listed in “Third-Party Services,” which may include the United States and other regions.

Recipients: Acorn Den and the third-party service providers we engage to process data on our behalf.

Method: Automated collection, transmission, storage, and statistical analysis. Support emails are read and answered by our staff.

Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party services, each of which processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Apple iCloud and In-App Purchase: syncing game progress and processing payments. See the Apple Privacy Policy;
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: crash and error reporting. See Privacy and Security in Firebase;
  • Unity Analytics (Unity Gaming Services): gameplay analytics. See the Unity Privacy Policy;
  • GitHub Pages: hosting our website and the Game’s announcement file. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Disclosure of Personal Information

We do not sell your data, nor do we provide it to advertisers or data brokers, except where required by law or by a competent authority.

Security

Connections between the Game and Firebase, Unity, and our website are encrypted with HTTPS, and we use data only for the purposes described in this Policy. If your personal data is stolen, leaked, altered, or otherwise compromised, we will notify you by appropriate means after investigating the matter, in accordance with Article 12 of the PDPA.

Data Retention

Game progress on your iPhone: Kept until you delete the Game or use “Delete Records” in Settings to start over.

Game progress in iCloud: Stored in your own iCloud. It is also cleared when you use “Delete Records” in Settings, and you can manage it in your iPhone’s iCloud settings.

Crash and error data: Kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days, after which deletion begins.

Gameplay usage: Raw event data in Unity Analytics is deleted automatically after 13 months. Aggregated figures derived from it that cannot identify individuals, such as daily player counts, may be kept longer.

Support emails: Kept until your request has been resolved. You may ask us to delete them at any time.

Your Rights

Under Article 3 of the PDPA, you may exercise the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  • to inquire about or request to review it;
  • to request a copy of it;
  • to request that it be supplemented or corrected;
  • to request that its collection, processing, or use be stopped;
  • to request that it be deleted.

To exercise these rights, please email support@acornden.com. Under Article 13 of the PDPA, we will handle requests to inquire, review, or obtain a copy within 15 days, and requests to supplement, correct, stop collection, processing, or use, or delete within 30 days. If necessary, we may extend each period once, by no more than 15 or 30 days respectively, and will notify you of the reason in writing.

Because the Game has no accounts, data held by Firebase and Unity is recorded under randomly generated installation identifiers that are not linked to your name or email address. As a result, we usually cannot tell which records belong to you. This data is deleted automatically when the periods in “Data Retention” expire. If you would still like to make a request, we will let you know what we can do and how.

You may also take the following steps yourself at any time: turn off step access in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness, or turn off notifications in Settings → Notifications; clear the body weight you entered on the Records page; turn off iCloud Sync in the Game’s Settings, or use “Delete Records” to clear your progress; or delete the Game, after which it will no longer collect any data.

If You Choose Not to Provide Data

You may decline step access or notifications and still play the Game normally; however, your steps will not be converted into rewards, and you will not receive exercise reminders. Body weight is optional; if you leave it blank, calories are estimated using a default value. If iCloud Sync is off, you will not be able to recover your progress after deleting the Game or changing phones.

Protection of Minors

The Game does not ask any user for a name, email address, or other identifying information, and the handling of data described in “Information We Collect and How We Use It” applies to users of all ages. Minors should obtain consent from a parent or guardian before making in-app purchases, and parents can use Apple’s Ask to Buy and Screen Time features to manage purchases. If you are a parent and find that your child has sent us personal information through “Contact Us,” please let us know and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Policy due to changes in law or in the Game. Revisions will be posted on this page with an updated date. For significant changes, such as collecting a new type of data, we will also notify you through an in-game announcement.
