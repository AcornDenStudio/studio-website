Stretching, napping, and every now and then sneaking out to find a little gift for its favorite person: you! When it gets hungry, a little bubble pops up over its head as it waits for dinner. And when it gets all dusty, it’s time for a nice, fresh bath together.

Pocket Shiba – Slow Jogging for a Healthier Life | Slow Jogging Timer is an iPhone game built around making slow jogging a habit, combined with raising your own Shiba Inu. It is free to download and offers in-app purchases. Your progress is stored on your iPhone by default, and you can choose to turn on iCloud Sync to keep it in your own iCloud.

經您同意後，本遊戲會讀取您的每日步數，僅用於換算成遊戲獎勵，不作其他用途。步數只在您的手機上處理，我們不會蒐集，也不會傳送您的步數資訊。

With your permission, the Game reads your daily step count and uses it only to convert into in-game rewards. Steps are processed only on your iPhone; we do not collect or transmit your step data.

遊戲中的卡路里依下列公式推估：

Calories in the Game are estimated with the following formula:

消耗熱量（大卡）＝ 代謝當量（METs）× 體重（公斤）× 運動時間（小時）

Calories (kcal) = METs × body weight (kg) × exercise time (hours)

本遊戲以 METs 4.8 計算；未填寫體重時，以 60 公斤估算。計算結果僅為近似值，本遊戲並非醫療或健身器材。詳細內容請參閱隱私權政策與使用條款。

The Game uses a METs value of 4.8; if you have not entered your weight, 60 kg is used. Results are approximate, and Pocket Shiba is not a medical or fitness device. For details, please see our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

常見問題FAQ

更換手機或刪除 App 後，遊戲進度還會在嗎？Will I keep my progress if I change phones or delete the app?

請先在遊戲的「設定」中開啟「iCloud 同步」（手機需登入 iCloud），進度就會同步一份到你本人的 iCloud，更換手機或重新安裝後都能找回。若未開啟，進度只會儲存在目前的手機上，刪除 App 後將無法復原。

Turn on “iCloud Sync” in the game’s Settings (your iPhone must be signed in to iCloud), and your progress will be synced to your own iCloud so you can recover it on a new phone or after reinstalling. If it is off, your progress is stored only on your current phone and cannot be recovered after the app is deleted.

購買的通行證或禮包不見了，該怎麼辦？What if my pass or bundle is missing?

請在「設定」中點選「還原購買」，即可找回地圖通行證與禮包中的限定道具。骨頭幣與消耗品使用後即消失，Apple 不提供還原，須透過 iCloud 同步保存。若仍無法找回，請來信與我們聯繫。

Tap “Restore Purchases” in Settings to recover map passes and exclusive items from bundles. Bone Coins and consumables are used up once spent and cannot be restored by Apple; they can only be kept through iCloud Sync. If something is still missing, please contact us.

如何申請退款？How do I request a refund?

App 內購買的付款與退款皆由 Apple 處理，我們無法直接辦理退款。申請方式如下：

  1. 前往 Apple 的回報問題網站，並登入你的 Apple 帳號。
  2. 選擇「申請退款」及原因，再勾選該筆購買。
  3. 送出申請後，Apple 將通知審核結果。

退款完成後，該次購買取得的內容將被收回，詳細規則請參閱使用條款。

Payments and refunds for in-app purchases are handled by Apple, so we cannot issue refunds directly. To request one:

  1. Go to Apple’s Report a Problem site and sign in with your Apple Account.
  2. Choose “Request a refund” and a reason, then select the purchase.
  3. Submit your request, and Apple will notify you of the result.

Once a refund is issued, the content from that purchase will be taken back. See our Terms of Use for details.

遊戲會讀取我的步數嗎？Does the game read my step count?

經你同意後，遊戲會讀取每日步數並換算為遊戲獎勵。步數只在你的手機上處理，不會傳送出去。你可以隨時在 iPhone 的「設定」→「隱私權與安全性」→「動作與健身」中關閉此權限。

With your permission, the game reads your daily step count and converts it into in-game rewards. Steps are processed only on your phone and are never sent anywhere. You can turn this off at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness.

一定要填寫體重嗎？Do I need to enter my weight?

不需要。體重為選填，僅用於將散步時間換算為卡路里；未填寫時，卡路里會以預設值估算。填寫的體重會隨遊戲進度儲存在你的手機（及你的 iCloud），不會傳送給我們，也可以隨時在紀錄頁清除。

No. Weight is optional and is used only to estimate calories from walk time; if you leave it blank, a default value is used. Your weight is saved with your progress on your phone (and in your iCloud), is never sent to us, and can be cleared at any time on the Records page.

抽獎券要如何使用？How do I use Lottery Tickets?

抽獎功能目前仍在準備中，你現在取得的抽獎券與碎片都會保留。抽獎券只能透過遊戲免費取得，無法以金錢購買。

The lottery feature is still in preparation, and any Lottery Tickets and fragments you collect now will be kept. Lottery Tickets can only be earned for free through gameplay and cannot be bought with money.
